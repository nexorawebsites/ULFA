04 From the Label

Body Mist · 150 ml

A lightweight fragrance for everyday wear — the complete product information, exactly as it appears on the packaging.

Certifications

PARABEN FREE · GMP · ISO · MADE IN LEBANON

Formulated to strict international quality standards, ensuring purity, safety, and an authentic botanical experience without compromise.

Ingredients

Water (Aqua), Alcohol Denat., Fragrance (Parfum), Glycerin, Polysorbate 20, Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice, Citric Acid, Sodium Benzoate, Potassium Sorbate, Tocopherol (Vitamin E), Limonene.

Directions

Spray generously onto the body from a distance of 15–20 cm. Reapply throughout the day as desired for an extra burst of freshness.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

Caution

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation or rash develops.
